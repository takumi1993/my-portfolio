how i work

01 / Inclusivity

Universal & Inclusive Design

Designing for everyone means leaving no one behind. Informed by real-world IoT and hardware products used by diverse demographics, I focus on low cognitive load, accessibility, and interfaces that feel instantly intuitive for all age groups.

02 / Behavior

Psychology-Driven UX

Intuitive interactions begin with understanding human behavior. Fascinated by applied and cognitive psychology, I critically examine mental models, habits, and subtle friction points before drawing the very first wireframe.

03 / Reflection

Reflective Thinking & Synthesis

Great design begins with continuous observation and reflection. I regularly deconstruct human behaviors, everyday interactions, and societal topics into written thoughts—training my ability to look beyond the surface before translating ideas into interfaces.
